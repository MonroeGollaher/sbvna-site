A lot-by-lot map of South Boise Village showing zoning, what the zoning allows (height, density, units and lot splits), current land use, assessed values and open city applications. It was made in response to the neighborhood’s designation as a federal Opportunity Zone, which takes effect January 1, 2027 and gives investors a tax incentive to build here. One of the map layers shows lots with potential development pressure for apartments, and why. The page loads the latest public data from the City of Boise and Ada County each time it opens. Choose what to show with “Color lots by,” and click any lot on the map or in the list for details.

How this page works

Live data. Each time the page opens it asks the City of Boise and Ada County map services for the neighborhood boundary, parcels, zoning, infill areas, development applications and streets. If those services can't be reached, it shows the copy saved on October 21, 2026 and says so at the top.

Why a lot may be targeted for apartments. Each lot gets at most one reason, checked in this order: commercial or vacant lots in MX-2/MX-3; houses already zoned MX-2/MX-3; MX-1 lots; R-2/R-3 lots; R-1C homes on 0.4 acre or more; vacant R-1C lots big enough to build on; whole platted lots with no house, found from the county’s legal descriptions; and tax-exempt land of a quarter acre or more. A few lots carry notes written by hand in October 2026.

Development pressure level. A lot with a reason is rated higher when it is not anyone’s primary residence (no homeowner’s exemption, or commercial, vacant or parking land), and highest when it is one of 3 or more such lots side by side in R-2, R-3 or mixed-use zoning. Owner-occupied lots, yard lots and tax-exempt land with a reason are rated moderate. This uses the exemption and lot lines only; owner names are never used.

"No homeowner's exemption." Lots without the Idaho homeowner's exemption are usually rentals or investor-owned. It is a rough signal of which owners might sell, not a statement about anyone's plans. An assessed value of $0 usually means a tax-exempt owner such as the city, a church or Boise State.

Limits. Being on this list means the zoning allows more than exists today. It does not mean a sale or project is planned. Owner names are not shown. Confirm anything important with Boise Planning & Development Services.
